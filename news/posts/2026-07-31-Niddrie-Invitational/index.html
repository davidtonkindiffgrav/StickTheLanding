Niddrie Invitational 2026: MAGA Sweeps Level 4 Division 1 as Edge Matches the Day's Top Score in Division 3

Niddrie fields four teams in a Level 3 Division 2 field decided by just 0.4 points, while Edge Gymnastics matches Melbourne Acrobatic Gymnastics Academy's best score of the competition to win Level 4 Division 3

31 July 2026  ·  Stick The Landing

TL;DR Gymnastics Victoria still hasn't published official results for the 11-12 July Niddrie Invitational at the time of publishing, so we reached out to Niddrie Gymnastic Club and Melbourne Acrobatic Gymnastics Academy (MAGA), and both clubs emailed over the official scoresheets. MAGA's Level 4 Division 1 team swept every apparatus and the all-around title. Its Level 4 Division 2 team backed that up with wins on bars, beam and floor, though Niddrie's third-placed team topped the vault, 27.6. Edge Gymnastics matched MAGA's best score of the day, an identical 28.7, to win Level 4 Division 3 outright. And in Level 3 Division 2, host club Niddrie fielded four separate teams in a field decided by just 0.4 points across the top three.

Levels 3 and 4 are judged as teams only, not individually, so this competition produced six divisional team results and no individual all-around field. It's also one of the slower results to land this season: Gymnastics Victoria hasn't published anything from it at the time of publishing, so we reached out directly. Both Niddrie Gymnastic Club and MAGA emailed over the full scoresheets, and the breakdown below is drawn straight from them.

MAGA'S LEVEL 4 SWEEP

Melbourne Acrobatic Gymnastics Academy's Level 4 Division 1 team won every apparatus and the all-around title: vault 28.15, bars 27.375, beam 27.15, floor 28.7, for a total of 111.375, three and a half points clear of second-placed Niddrie on 107.875. That floor score, 28.7, is the highest single mark posted anywhere in the competition.

Its Level 4 Division 2 team backed that up, winning bars (27.55, the best bars rotation of the day), beam (26.95) and floor (28.625) on the way to the division's all-around title on 108.875. The one apparatus it didn't win was vault, where Niddrie's third-placed team posted the field's best mark, 27.6, but MAGA's strong showing on floor made up for it: at 28.625, it's the second-best floor mark of the entire competition, behind only its own Division 1 team's 28.7.

EDGE MATCHES THE DAY'S TOP SCORE

Edge Gymnastics won Level 4 Division 3 outright on 107.85, built on a vault score of 28.7, identical to MAGA's floor mark and the only other score in the competition to reach it, plus a beam score of 27.525, the best beam rotation of the day. Strive Gymnastics fielded three teams in the same division and finished 2nd, 4th and 5th, with its highest-placed team just 2.475 points behind Edge on 105.375.

NIDDRIE'S HOME-FLOOR DEPTH

Host club Niddrie didn't need a division win to make its presence felt. In Level 3 Division 2, the club fielded four separate teams, finishing 4th, 5th, 7th and 10th in a field of ten, while the division itself produced the tightest team finish of the competition: MAGA won it on 106.6, just 0.175 ahead of LFK Dance and Gym on 106.425, with Edge a further 0.225 back on 106.2. Less than half a point covered the top three.

LFK also turned in the second-best bars rotation of the entire competition, 27.5 in Level 3 Division 3 on its way to that division's win, just 0.05 shy of MAGA's day-best 27.55 in Level 4 Division 2.

A DAY AT THE VENUE

I was at Niddrie in person for day two, the three Level 4 divisions. The competition was well run: the schedule wasn't overloaded, sessions started and finished on time, and the club made good use of a fairly compact venue, including a small canteen, appreciated by the second session of the day. I'll be looking forward to it being back on the calendar next year.

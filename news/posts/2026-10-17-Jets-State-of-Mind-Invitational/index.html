2026 Jets State of Mind Invitational: One Last Hit-Out Before Vics

MAGA's Lara Lockwood and Ivy Drake post season bests to win Level 5 Divisions 1 and 2, Evie Shinkfield and River Morgan share gold in Level 6 Division 2, and Indigo Gration leads a MAGA top seven in Level 7 Division 1

David "Tonko" Tonkin  ·  17 October 2026
TL;DR The 2026 Jets State of Mind Invitational on 10 October was the last competition before the Victorian Championships. MAGA's Lara Lockwood won Level 5 Division 1 on 37.275 ahead of Jets' Elena Yeo, and clubmate Ivy Drake won Level 5 Division 2 on 36.55, both their best scores of the 2026 season. In Level 6 Division 2, Jets' Evie Shinkfield and Aerodynamix's River Morgan shared gold on 35.375, and in Level 7 Division 1, Indigo Gration led MAGA to the top seven scores on 47.4. In Levels 3 and 4, MAGA and PIT took three team titles each.

We know, everyone wants the Victorian Championships results, and with Vics happening right now, this weekend, we're right there with you. But we can't forget about the invitationals, so here's a quick wrap-up of the Jets State of Mind Invitational on 10 October. We promise to get the Victorian Championships results, and a wrap-up article, up as soon as we get them.

Before we get into it, can we just take a moment to acknowledge the word play of this comp? "State of Mind" and it's the last comp before States... I see what you did there Jets... and as a Dad who loves a good Dad-Joke... I'm impressed.

MAGA AND PIT SHARE THE LEVEL 3 AND 4 TEAM TITLES

MAGA won the team title in Level 3 Division 1, Level 3 Division 2 and Level 4 Division 1, where MAGA Pink's 113.0 was the highest team score of the day across Levels 3 and 4. PIT matched them with three of its own, PIT Blue in Level 3 Division 3, PIT Aqua in Level 4 Division 3, and PIT Blue again in one of the two Level 4 Division 2 fields. Aerodynamix Blue took the other on 108.525. In Level 3 Division 1, Geelong YMCA Black and Geelong YMCA Red took second and third.

SEASON BESTS AT THE TOP OF LEVEL 5

Lara Lockwood won Level 5 Division 1 on 37.275, her best score of the 2026 season, ahead of Jets' Elena Yeo on 37.15, also a season best, with MAGA's Luckshetha Mauran just 0.025 further back in bronze. Only 0.15 separated the top three.

Level 5 Division 2 was the biggest field of the day at thirty-six gymnasts, and MAGA's Ivy Drake won it on 36.55, close to a full point above her previous best this season. Clubmate Bella-Maree Moxon was second on 36.475 and Jets' Felicity Ford third on 36.35, both season bests as well. Level 5 Division 3 ran as two fields, won by MYC's Charlotte Rawlings (33.825) and Athleta's Zoe Mencia Pham (34.9).

A SHARED GOLD IN LEVEL 6

Jets' Evie Shinkfield and Aerodynamix' River Morgan couldn't be split in Level 6 Division 2, sharing gold on 35.375, a season best for both. Jets Diamond Creek won the team title ahead of Jets Gisborne. In Division 1, Jets' Jovie Dirkx won on 35.625 ahead of Geelong YMCA's Sharmaine Jinnie Kon, who took gold on vault and floor, and in Division 3, PIT's Freya O'Reilly took gold on 33.575.

GRATION LEADS A MAGA TOP SEVEN IN LEVEL 7

Indigo Gration won Level 7 Division 1 on 47.4, leading MAGA to the top seven all-around scores and a team total of 141.3, with Geelong YMCA second on 135.5. Level 7 Division 2 ran as two fields. In one, MAGA's Liza Hyndman and Geelong YMCA's Evie Lambert shared gold on 45.15, and Geelong YMCA finished just 0.4 behind MAGA for the team title. In the other, EKGA's Sienna Caputa posted a season best 45.0 to lead an EKGA one-two-three, ahead of Harriet Gluschenko and MacKenzie Barbara.

ONE LAST QUIET WEEKEND

Once Vics wraps up, we're all waiting to see how things look after the dust settles on the major changes Gymnastics Australia is bringing in. Nobody knows the exact details yet, but the common belief is that things are going to get harder for the athletes, with harder skills at the lower levels, balanced by more choice of skills and updated scoring for Levels 5 and 6. So let's enjoy this one, the last article before all hell breaks loose. Good luck to everyone competing at Vics this weekend.

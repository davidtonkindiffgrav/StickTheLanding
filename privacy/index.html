Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Stick The Landing (stickthelanding.com.au) is a free, independent leaderboard for Victorian artistic gymnastics. This policy explains how we handle data and what third-party services operate on the site.

Competition results data

This site collates and displays athlete names, club affiliations, and competition scores sourced from official, publicly published competition results. This data is the same information made available by Gymnastics Victoria and affiliated bodies through their own publications and result sheets.

We do not require users to create an account, log in, or submit any personal details to view results on this site.

Profile images

Athlete profile pages may display a profile photograph alongside an athlete's name and competition results. Profile images are only published where explicit consent has been provided through our submission form.

By submitting a profile image, the submitter declares and agrees to the following:

"I am 16 or over, or I am the legal guardian of the person in this picture. I give permission for this profile image to be displayed next to the name associated with this athlete profile. I understand that the image may be cropped to fit the website."

Profile images submitted on behalf of athletes under 16 will only be published where the above consent has been provided by a parent or legal guardian. We reserve the right to decline or remove any image at our discretion.

To request the removal of a profile image, please contact us at dptonkin@gmail.com. Removal requests will be processed promptly.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, including pages visited, session duration, device type, and approximate location (country/city level). This data is used to understand how the site is being used and to improve it over time. No personally identifying information is collected through Analytics.

For more information on how Google handles Analytics data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense

This site displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and similar technologies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data when you visit sites that use its advertising services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Cookies

Cookies on this site are set by Google Analytics and Google AdSense. We do not set any first-party cookies ourselves. Google Analytics uses cookies (such as _ga) to distinguish users and track sessions anonymously. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve relevant advertisements. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; doing so will not affect your ability to view competition results or leaderboards on this site.

Third-party links

Articles on this site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies independently.

Children's privacy

This site displays competition results that may include the names of athletes under the age of 18. This information is sourced exclusively from official, publicly published competition results and is treated no differently from results published by the governing bodies themselves.

We do not solicit or knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Profile images for athletes under 16 are only accepted where a parent or legal guardian has provided explicit consent through our submission form, as described above.

If you believe information about a child has been published in error or wish to request its removal, please contact us at dptonkin@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at dptonkin@gmail.com.

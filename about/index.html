About Stick The Landing

Stick The Landing is an independent, free resource for the Victorian gymnastics community. We collate official competition results across Women's Artistic Gymnastics (WAG) and Men's Artistic Gymnastics (MAG), turning individual score PDFs into live season leaderboards - updated throughout the year after every competition.

The site covers every level of the Victorian competitive season, from development competitions through to State Trials and the Border Challenge. Gymnasts, coaches, clubs, and families can track all-around standings, apparatus rankings, and team results in one place.

How results get in

Results are automatically parsed from official Gymnastics Victoria score PDFs. If you spot a discrepancy - a missing result, an incorrect score, or a name entered differently across competitions - please get in touch and we'll fix it.

News and articles

Alongside the leaderboards, we publish competition reports, analysis, and community updates covering the Victorian season. Content is written by people who care about the sport.

Independence

Stick The Landing is not affiliated with Gymnastics Victoria or any club. It is a community resource built and maintained independently.

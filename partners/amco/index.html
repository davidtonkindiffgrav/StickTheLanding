What is Stick The Landing?

The #1 place for coaches, families and gymnasts to find their results

A FREE, independent home for Victorian WAG and MAG competition results, searchable in seconds, with no login required.

Season leaderboardsEvery level and apparatus, ranked by season-best score and updated after each competition.
Athlete profilesEvery result a gymnast has recorded since 2024, with graphs to track their progress.
Find a gymnastSearch by name and jump straight to any gymnast's results.
Competition reportsWrite-ups covering the close finishes, standout performances and storylines.
Club profilesIn-depth features on Victorian clubs, their history and their gymnasts.
Promotion WatchFollowing which teams and gymnasts have cleared the scores that could see them move up.
Who is Stick The Landing?

Built by a gymnastics family, for the gymnastics community

I'm David, a former MAG gymnast, and now a gymnastics dad. Gymnastics has never really left my family: my mother started her own gym club in my hometown, my sister was a Rhythmic Gymnastics Australian Champion, and my own daughter now trains close to twenty hours a week as a Division 1 WAG gymnast.

After many years away from the sport, Stick The Landing is my way of putting something back into a community that, because of my daughter's love of gymnastics, has once again become a major part of my family's life. Every gymnast in the database, whatever their level, deserves to feel like their results matter.

Here's what I built.

Every 2026 competition on the site

Results are compiled and published after each competition, sourced from official Gymnastics Victoria result documents.

Behind the scenes, custom software reads the many different PDF formats Gymnastics Victoria publishes results in and pulls every score straight into the database, so nobody has to dig through dozens of separate documents to find the result they're after.

"A FREE, unified, searchable database covering multiple seasons, thousands of athletes, and tens of thousands of scores, something that doesn't exist anywhere else in Australian club gymnastics."

But Stick The Landing has a problem…

The 2026 season is over, and in 2027 I'll be handling more comps, more athletes and more results.

Right now, Stick The Landing runs on free static hosting. It's worked well to get here, but it wasn't built to keep scaling with a growing database. Moving to a secure, paid hosting plan is what keeps the site fast, reliable, and free for every gymnastics family in Victoria.

And this is just the beginning.

Beyond Victoria

Work is already underway on expanding this same free, unified results experience into other states, starting with New South Wales.

As Stick The Landing grows into new states, so does the audience seeing your brand, from one state's gymnastics community to a genuinely national one.

Who is Stick The Landing?

Built by a gymnastics family, for the gymnastics community

I'm David, a former MAG gymnast, and now a gymnastics dad. Gymnastics has never really left my family: my mother started her own gym club in my hometown, my sister was a Rhythmic Gymnastics Australian Champion, and my own daughter now trains close to twenty hours a week as a Division 1 WAG gymnast.

After many years away from the sport, Stick The Landing is my way of putting something back into a community that, because of my daughter's love of gymnastics, has once again become a major part of my family's life. Every gymnast in the database, whatever their level, deserves to feel like their results matter.

Here's what I built.

Every result, in one place

Score trends, apparatus by apparatus
Competition-by-competition breakdowns

Results are compiled and published after each competition, sourced from official Gymnastics Victoria result documents.

Behind the scenes, custom software reads the many different PDF formats Gymnastics Victoria publishes results in and pulls every score straight into the database, so nobody has to dig through dozens of separate documents to find the result they're after.

"A unified, searchable database covering multiple seasons, thousands of athletes, and tens of thousands of scores, something that doesn't exist anywhere else in Australian club gymnastics."
